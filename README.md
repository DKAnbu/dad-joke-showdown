## The joke

> I told my socks they were a perfect pair, and now they refuse to split up even in the laundry.

A dad at the dinner table tells his socks they make a perfect pair — and the socks take him literally, refusing to split up even in the laundry. The pun is "perfect pair": a matching pair of socks, and a couple that belongs together. One sentence, 94 characters, punchline at the end.

## How to run

Requires Node.js 22 or newer.

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the single dad joke (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.
